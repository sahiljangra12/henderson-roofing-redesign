Our History

Six decades overhead

1963

The Beginning

John van Spronsen, a young entrepreneur who immigrated from Holland, learns the roofing trade and founds Bond-A-Ply Roofing Ltd.

1974

DM Henderson Roofing

John expands the operation and purchases DM Henderson Roofing. His passion, vision and integrity build it into the reputable business it is today.

Today

Full-Service Firm

Roofing, waterproofing and sheet metal fabrication across the residential, commercial, institutional and industrial sectors.
